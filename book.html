About This Adventure
Explore the experience before booking

Lingya Waterfall is a beautiful natural attraction surrounded by lush greenery and vibrant monsoon landscapes. The experience combines trekking, nature, swimming and adventurous rappelling.

The area is ideal for participants who enjoy nature, adventure, photography and exploring less-crowded destinations.

Major Attractions
Highlights of the expedition
300+ ft adventurous rappelling.
Beautiful monsoon waterfall experience.
Natural pool below the waterfall.
Crystal-clear bluish-green water.
Dense forest and scenic surroundings.
