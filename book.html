🌿 Brief Information About Lingya Waterfall

Name: Lingya Waterfall. Lingya Ghat Waterfall is a stunning natural attraction located just 13.5 km ahead of Lavasa city in Maharashtra. The waterfall derives its name from the prominent pinnacle nearby, known as Lingya, which holds significant cultural and spiritual value for the local community. This striking formation is often revered and worshipped by the villagers of Dhamanohol, the base village for the waterfall. Surrounded by lush greenery and vibrant flora, the waterfall is especially breathtaking during the monsoon season when it transforms into a powerful cascade, drawing visitors for its scenic beauty and serene atmosphere. The area is perfect for nature enthusiasts, offering opportunities for trekking, birdwatching and photography.
