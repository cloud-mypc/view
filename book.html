About This Adventure

Lingya Waterfall is a stunning natural attraction located around 13.5 km ahead of Lavasa city in Maharashtra.

The waterfall derives its name from the prominent pinnacle nearby, known as Lingya, which holds cultural and spiritual value for the local community.

Surrounded by lush greenery and vibrant flora, the waterfall becomes especially breathtaking during the monsoon season.

Major Attractions
Most beautiful waterfall experience in Maharashtra.
300+ ft adventurous rappelling.
Natural pool below the waterfall.
Crystal-clear bluish-green water.
Dense forest exploration.
