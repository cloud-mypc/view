🌿 About Lingya Waterfall

Lingya Waterfall is a stunning natural attraction located around 13.5 km ahead of Lavasa city in Maharashtra.

The waterfall derives its name from the prominent pinnacle nearby, known as Lingya, which has significant cultural and spiritual value for the local community.

Surrounded by lush greenery and vibrant flora, the waterfall becomes especially breathtaking during the monsoon season.

The area offers an excellent combination of trekking, nature, photography, swimming and adventurous rappelling.
